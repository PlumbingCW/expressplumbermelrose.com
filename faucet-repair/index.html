Emergency Faucet Repair in Melrose, MA

A faucet that will not stop flowing is an emergency in any kitchen or bathroom, so we pick up 24/7. Faucet repair covers the handle, cartridge, spout and supply valves that control water at a sink, tub or outdoor spigot.

Look for the small oval or round valve under the sink and turn it clockwise. If it will not move, close the main water shutoff and open the lowest faucet in the house to relieve pressure. Set a bucket and towel under any drip.

A valve that will not shut off

Most faucet calls are a drip. The urgent ones are a handle that spins without stopping flow, a spout that cracked, or an angle valve that snaps off in your hand. When the little valve under the sink fails, the only way to stop the water may be the main.

  • ✓
    Gushing at the spout. The handle has stripped or the cartridge has broken. Close the valves below the sink or the main.
  • ✓
    Spray from under the basin. A supply line or its valve has failed. Shut the main and cut power to nearby outlets if water is pooling.
  • ✓
    Valve will not turn. Old stops seize with mineral buildup. Forcing one can snap the stem, so stop and call.
  • ✓
    Water behind a wall. Wet drywall near a tub or sink faucet means the leak is inside the wall. Shut the main and photograph the damage.
  • ✓
    Outdoor spigot dripping inside. A hose bib that leaks into the wall is common after a cold snap. Close its interior valve if you can reach it.

For a pipe that has burst rather than a fixture, go to our burst pipe repair page.

Aerators, flushing and Melrose water

The city's water letter tells residents to remove faucet aerators every few months and run the water 3 to 5 minutes. The city gives this as part of its lead guidance, and it also clears grit that can slow the flow. If your flow has dropped to a trickle, a clogged aerator is the first cheap check.

Melrose also flushes its mains, and in 2025 the program ran from September 8 into late October. The city warns that sediment can clog home pipes and reduce pressure. If a faucet sputters or runs rusty after flushing, run cold water until it clears, then clean the aerator before calling us.

Source type: Melrose DPW water quality guidance and 2025 flushing notice
