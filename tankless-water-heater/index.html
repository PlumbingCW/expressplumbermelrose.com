Tankless Water Heater Service in Melrose, MA

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows through the unit, so there is no storage tank to run out. Service covers flushing and descaling, error codes, leaks, sensors and installation of a new on-demand unit.

What we do on a tankless unit

Flush and descale

We circulate a descaling solution through the heat exchanger to dissolve mineral buildup, then rinse and clean the inlet filter.

Error codes and beeping

Codes point to a sensor, a flame fault, a vent problem or a flow issue. We read the code, test the part and fix the cause.

Leaks

A leak may be a loose fitting, a failed valve or a cracked heat exchanger. We find the source and tell you if the unit is worth keeping.

Replacement and install

We size a new unit for your fixtures, check gas line capacity and vent, and swap or add the unit.

Why scale is the main tankless enemy

Minerals in the water bake onto the hot surfaces of the heat exchanger and shrink the passages. The unit then runs hotter, trips codes and heats less. The Melrose water report from MWRA gives no hardness figure, so test your tap or watch for white crust on fixtures to judge how fast scale forms in your home.

How a tankless flush works

  1. 1

    Power down and isolate

    We shut off the gas and power, then close the water isolation valves on the unit.

  2. 2

    Connect the pump

    A small pump and hoses connect to the service ports, so the cleaning solution can run in a loop.

  3. 3

    Circulate

    The solution runs through the heat exchanger for a set time, loosening scale.

  4. 4

    Rinse and restore

    We flush with clean water, clean the inlet filter, reopen the valves and confirm the unit fires and holds temperature.

Flush frequency depends on your water and the manufacturer's schedule. If your unit has never been flushed, plan one soon.

Signs your tankless needs attention

    Lukewarm waterThe outlet temperature will not reach the setting, even with low flow.
  • ✓
    Beeping or a code on the displayThe unit is reporting a fault, a vent problem or a flow issue.
  • ✓
    Loud rumbling or whistlingScale narrows the passages, which makes noise.
  • ✓
    Water pooling under the unitA fitting or heat exchanger may be leaking. Shut off the water to the unit.
  • ✓
    Scorched or burnt wiresStop using the unit and call. Do not touch exposed wiring.
