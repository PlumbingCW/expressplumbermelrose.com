Why East Side houses call for planned plumbing

The survey names Upham Street, Porter Street and Grove Street, and places revival designs near the Bellevue Golf Course. Wikipedia says the East Side was mostly developed in the early 1900s. Residents draw the edges differently, so give your street address when you call.

A large house may have several bathrooms, a kitchen, a laundry and sometimes a basement or attic bath. Each one adds supply lines, valves and drains. If a plumber is already there for one leaking faucet, a look at the valves under every other sink adds little time.

Water heater capacity for a house with several bathrooms

One tank feeds every shower, sink and appliance. In a house with three or four bathrooms, two showers plus a dishwasher can empty a small tank quickly. If hot water runs out on a normal morning, capacity may be the issue, not a failing heater.

Sizing depends on the busiest hour of demand, not the gallon label alone. Tell us what runs at once and we can discuss a larger tank, a high-recovery model or a tankless water heater. An aging tank is better swapped on your schedule, and our water heater replacement page explains how. In a double house, ask early whether each unit has its own heater.

Single house or double house: what changes

The 1981 survey describes double-bay double houses on Upham Street. A double holds two households in one building. This comparison is general plumbing information for any two-family house.

In a double house

  • Shared walls, basement space or a stack mean a leak can reach a neighbor
  • Shutting water off may need coordination with the other unit
  • Meter and shutoff locations need sorting out before work starts

In a single-family house

  • One owner decides timing and scope
  • The main shutoff and heater serve one household
  • Easier to group fixtures into one project

If you own one side of a double, tell us which side and floor. Renters should ask the owner before any work on shared pipes.

Gas smell or water near the electric panel

If you smell gas near a gas water heater or boiler, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility before you call a plumber. Do not use switches or light anything.

If water is reaching the electric panel or outlets, stay away from it and shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely. Then call a plumber and an electrician.
