How we choose where to go

We list the nearby towns we serve from our Melrose base. A call from one of these towns goes to the same 24/7 line, and the dispatcher books the soonest plumber available.

Not every address inside a town is equally close to us, so we confirm timing on the phone rather than promise a figure here. If you live outside the five towns listed, call anyway and we will tell you plainly whether we can come.

Before you call, it helps to have the street address, the town and a short description of the problem ready. Tell the dispatcher if water is actively running, if there is a gas smell or if a basement is taking on water. That order of questions lets us decide what to send and what you should do while you wait.
