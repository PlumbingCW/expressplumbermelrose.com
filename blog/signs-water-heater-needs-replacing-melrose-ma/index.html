Signs your water heater needs replacing in Melrose, MA

The clearest signs are water pooling at the base of the tank, rusty or cloudy hot water, loud rumbling or popping, and a tank that is more than about 10 years old. Any one of them can mean repair is enough, but a leak from the tank itself almost always means replacement. A tank cannot be patched once the steel wall has failed.

This guide covers each warning sign, a repair-or-replace table, and what to expect in Melrose, including how city water main flushing can look like a failing heater when it is not.

Seven warning signs

Water around the base

Wet floor under the tank is the one sign you should not wait on. A leak from a fitting or valve can be repaired. A leak through the tank wall cannot. Shut off the cold supply and the power or gas and look at where the water starts.

Rusty hot water

If rust-colored water comes out of hot taps only, the tank interior or the anode rod may be corroding. If the cold taps run rusty too, the cause is likely upstream. See the Melrose note below before you decide.

Rumbling or popping

Sediment settles at the bottom, traps water under it and boils it into noise. A flush may help. If the sediment is thick and the noise continues, the tank is working too hard and wasting fuel.

Age past ten years

Most storage tanks last roughly 8 to 12 years, and that varies with water quality and care. Find the date on the data plate. An older heater that starts misbehaving is a replacement candidate.

Not enough hot water

Shorter showers can come from a failed heating element, a bad thermostat, or a tank full of sediment. A newer tank with this issue often needs a repair. An old one often needs replacing.

Pilot or burner trouble

A pilot that will not stay lit, a yellow flame, or soot around the burner needs attention. A bad thermocouple is a cheap fix. A cracked heat exchanger or draft problem is not.

Metallic taste or smell

A rotten egg smell from hot water only often points to a reaction in the tank. A metallic taste can come from corrosion. Both are worth a look when the tank is also old.

One sign on a young heater is a repair conversation. Two or three on an old one is a replacement conversation.

How we think about repair versus replacement

Start with the leak test. Dry the area, wrap a paper towel around the suspect fitting and see where it gets wet. A drip from the pipe connections or relief valve is a repair for water heater repair service. Water from the tank seam or bottom is not.

Next, ask about parts and cost. An electric element or thermostat is inexpensive. A gas control valve on an older tank can cost nearly as much as a new unit. Efficiency matters too. Modern heaters are better insulated, which lowers standby loss, so a water heater replacement can pay for part of itself over time. How much depends on your usage.

Melrose note: rusty water, main flushing and permits

The City of Melrose ran citywide water main flushing in 2025 from Monday, September 8 into late October, generally 8 am to 4 pm. During flushing the city warns that water may look rusty or cloudy, to run cold water until it clears, and to hold off on hot water, laundry and the dishwasher until it does.

If your water turned rusty while the city was flushing, test the cold tap first. If cold is rusty too, it is probably the main and not your heater.

Melrose gets its water from MWRA, which adjusts pH and buffering to reduce corrosion in pipes. That helps, but it does not stop an old tank from rusting out from the inside. On the permit side, the city's fee schedule lists a water heater at $40, and a residential gas water heater at $40 as well. Ask your plumber who applies for the permit and when the inspection is scheduled.

What to do the day you suspect a failing heater

  1. 1

    Check for water

    Look at the floor and the tank base with a flashlight. Photograph any wet area.

  2. 2

    Shut off the energy source

    Turn gas control to off or flip the breaker for electric. Never leave a leaking electric tank powered.

  3. 3

    Close the cold inlet

    The shutoff on the cold line stops more water from filling the tank.

  4. 4

    Note the data plate

    Take a photo of the model, serial and capacity. It speeds up quoting.

  5. 5

    Call and describe

    Tell us what you see and the heater age. If the floor is flooding, say so.

If you smell gas, do not stay to troubleshoot. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.
