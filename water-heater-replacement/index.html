Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Melrose, MA

Water heater replacement means removing a failed tank, fitting a new one, and reconnecting the water, fuel or power, venting and safety valves. If your tank has burst, is leaking hard or has left you without hot water, call our 24/7 line and we will tell you the soonest available slot.

Failed tank flooding the basement: first moves

Water from a burst tank can reach a floor drain, a finished room or a utility panel quickly. Shut the cold supply on the pipe above the heater, then turn the gas control to off or trip the breaker for an electric heater. If you smell gas, go outside and call your gas utility or 911 first.

Keep the main water valve in mind as a backup. If the heater valve will not turn, closing the main stops the flow into the house. Then call us and tell the dispatcher the fuel type, the tank size on the label and where the unit sits.

Replace now or wait? A general age and symptom guide

The 8 to 12 year range is a general rule of thumb for storage tank life. Water, usage and maintenance can move it either way.

What you seeWhat it usually meansTypical call
Water leaking from the tank shellLining has failedReplace
Rust streaks or wet insulation at the baseCorrosion has startedReplace soon
Tank over 12 years old with no hot waterParts may fail againReplace, price a repair first
Drip at a valve or fitting on a younger tankA part has wornRepair

How a replacement visit goes

  1. 1

    Confirm the fit

    We check the tank size, fuel type, vent path and available space, then match a replacement to your household's hot water use.

  2. 2

    Drain and disconnect

    The old tank is isolated, drained to a floor drain or outside, and disconnected from the water, gas or electric and vent.

  3. 3

    Set and connect

    The new heater goes on a stable base or pan. We connect new supply lines, a new T&P valve and its discharge pipe, and the fuel and vent.

  4. 4

    Fill, test and light

    We fill the tank, purge air from the lines, check every joint for leaks, light a gas unit or power an electric one, and confirm it reaches temperature.

  5. 5

    Remove the old unit

    The old tank is taken away so it does not sit in your garage or basement.

Melrose permit fees and who pulls the permit

The City of Melrose lists a $40 plumbing fee for a water heater, and a $40 residential gas fee for a gas water heater. Work started without a permit is charged at triple the fee, so ask who is handling it.

Ask Melrose Inspectional Services whether a like-for-like swap needs a permit in Melrose. A licensed plumber normally pulls any permit that is needed, and you can check with them at 781-979-4135. Plumbing and gas inspections happen Monday to Thursday, and the printed permit is posted at the job site.

What sets the price of a new water heater

Tank size and efficiencyA larger tank or a high-efficiency model costs more than a basic 40 or 50 gallon unit.
Fuel typeGas, electric and hybrid heat pump units differ in unit cost and in what they need to run.
Venting and gas lineMoving a vent, adding a liner or changing a gas connector adds labor and parts.
Code upgradesExpansion tanks, a drain pan, new shutoff valves or a corrected discharge pipe may be needed.
Access and timingA tight attic, a basement stair or an overnight visit changes the labor involved.
Insurance note. Sudden tank failure that causes water damage is often handled under a homeowner's policy as a separate claim from the heater itself. Gas, electric or hybrid: what usually fits

Most homes replace what they have. A switch is possible, but it changes the work.

Like for like

  • Fastest path on an emergency call
  • Existing gas line, vent and power stay in place
  • Fewer parts to source
  • Lower surprise cost

Switching types

  • Gas to electric or hybrid needs a suitable circuit and space
  • Hybrid units need clearance for airflow and a condensate drain
  • A tankless swap changes venting and gas sizing
  • Better as a planned install than an emergency fix

If you want to explore a change, see water heater installation for planned work, or our Wakefield service area page if you live nearby.
