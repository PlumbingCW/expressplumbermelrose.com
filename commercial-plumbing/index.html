Commercial services we handle

Grease trap cleaning

A trap catches fats and oils before they harden in the sewer. Many jurisdictions expect it cleaned when it is a quarter full. Check your own requirement with the authority that regulates your building, and we set a pump schedule that matches.

Floor drain service

Floor drains back up when debris builds or the trap dries out and lets sewer gas rise. We clear the line, flush the trap and, where needed, install a trap primer.

Backflow testing

A backflow assembly keeps dirty water from flowing back into the clean supply. Testing schedules and who receives the report are set by your water supplier and the state. Ask the Melrose water office what applies to your assembly. Melrose's plumbing fee schedule includes a $25 line for a residential backflow prevention device. Ask the city what applies to a commercial assembly.

Commercial leak detection

Abnormal meter readings, wet parking lots or a rising water bill point to hidden leaks. We use listening and electronic tools, then repair what we find. See leak detection for how the process works.
