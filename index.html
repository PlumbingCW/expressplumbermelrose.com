What counts as a plumbing emergency

Some problems can wait for a weekday appointment, and some cannot. A burst pipe, sewage coming up a floor drain, a gas smell, a water heater leaking onto the floor and a toilet that will not stop overflowing all belong to the second group.

As a rule, if water is still spreading or a fixture is unusable, treat it as urgent and call the emergency plumber line. A slow drip under a sink can usually hold until morning if you place a bucket under it and close the fixture's shutoff valve.

If it is a gas smell

Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Call a plumber after the building is safe. Never flip switches or light anything while you are inside.

Before we arrive

What to do in the first five minutes

These steps limit the damage while a plumber is on the way.

  1. 1

    Stop the water

    Close the main shutoff valve, usually near the water meter in the basement. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

  2. 2

    Cut power near water

    If water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, switch off power to that area only if you can reach the breaker while standing on dry ground.

  3. 3

    Drain the lines

    Open a faucet at the lowest level of the house to relieve pressure, and open the highest one to let air in.

  4. 4

    Take photos

    Photograph the damage and the source before you clean up. Your insurer or landlord will ask for them.

  5. 5

    Call us

    Give the address, what you see and where the shutoff is. Then keep the area clear for the plumber.

Melrose water, permits and what they mean for your repair

Melrose buys its drinking water directly from the MWRA, so what comes out of your tap starts as Quabbin and Wachusett Reservoir water and is treated with monochloramine as a disinfectant. The city's water letter advises removing faucet aerators every few months and running the water for 3 to 5 minutes, so it is worth making that a habit.

Plumbing and gas permits in Melrose are issued by Inspectional Services at City Hall, 562 Main Street, and applied for through the city's online portal. Staff review can take two to three weeks, so a planned job like a bathroom remodel is best scheduled ahead. For a water heater swap, ask Inspectional Services whether a permit applies. Ask your plumber whether your job needs a permit and who pulls it.

The city flushes its water mains from time to time, and in 2025 the program ran from September 8 into late October. Flushing can leave water cloudy or rusty, so run the cold tap until it clears before you decide something is wrong with your own pipes.
