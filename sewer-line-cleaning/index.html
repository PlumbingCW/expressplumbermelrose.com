Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Melrose, MA

If sewage is coming up in a shower, toilet or basement drain, the main line is likely blocked. Call our 24/7 line, stop all water use and keep people away from the affected room until a plumber clears it.

Sewer line cleaning is the clearing of the main drain that carries all of a home's wastewater to the street. It uses a heavy cable machine or a water jet to remove roots, grease and debris.

Main line clog or sewage backing up

A main line clog backs up the lowest drains first. That is usually a basement floor drain, a basement bathroom or a ground-floor tub. Upstairs fixtures often seem fine until water use rises.

Once the main is blocked, anything you flush or drain adds to the problem. The best thing you can do is stop the flow, open a window for ventilation and wait for the plumber. If a floor drain is already overflowing, move belongings out of the way.

Gloves and boots matter if you have to touch the water. Sewage carries bacteria, so wash hands well afterward and keep children and pets in another room.

How a main line cleaning visit runs

  1. 1

    Find the access

    The plumber locates the cleanout or opens a fixture that gives access to the main.

  2. 2

    Run the cable or jet

    A heavy cable cuts through roots and debris. If grease or buildup is the cause, a water jet follows.

  3. 3

    Flush and test

    Water runs through the line to prove flow, and the plumber checks that the lowest drain stays dry.

  4. 4

    Camera and next steps

    The line is inspected on screen, and any damage or repeat cause is explained before any further work.

Grease trap cleaning for kitchens and small shops

A grease trap catches fat before it reaches the sewer. When it fills, grease passes through and lines the pipes downstream, which causes backups in kitchens and the line to the street. A trap that smells or drains slowly needs pumping.

Restaurants and shops usually handle trap pumping on a schedule. If your trap is in a building you own, a plumber can inspect it, clean the lines on either side and tell you how often it needs service. For heavier buildup, hydro jetting clears the line the trap feeds.

Why the lowest drain shows it first

Water in a blocked main finds the lowest opening, which is why the basement floor drain or a basement shower overflows before a second-floor sink does. The level in the pipe rises until it reaches that drain.

That is also why a floor drain backing up on a dry day points to the line, not the fixture. Pouring more water down the sink only adds to the pile, so hold off until the main is open.
