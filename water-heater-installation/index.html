Tank versus tankless: choose before you buy

The biggest early decision is whether to store hot water or heat it on demand.

Storage tank

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Simple to replace later
  • Runs out when the tank empties
  • Takes floor space

Tankless

  • Heats as you use water
  • Smaller footprint, wall mounted
  • Needs correct gas line sizing and venting
  • Needs periodic descaling

Our tankless water heater page covers on-demand units in detail.

What we check at your house before quoting

A heater is only as good as the space around it. These items shape the work.

  • ✓
    Fuel and supplyIs there a gas line near enough, or an electric circuit with spare capacity in the panel?
  • ✓
    Vent pathA gas unit needs a proper flue or vent route. A change in type can change the vent.
  • ✓
    Space and clearanceHybrid units need airflow around them and a place for condensate to drain.
  • ✓
    Drain and panA floor drain or pan helps if the tank ever fails above living space.
  • ✓
    Existing pipingOld shutoffs, corroded fittings or worn supply lines are cheaper to renew while the heater is open.
  • ✓
    Household demandThe number of bathrooms and people sets the tank size or the flow a tankless unit needs.

How an installation runs

  1. 1

    Site visit and quote

    We look at the space, fuel, vent and demand, then explain options and price.

  2. 2

    Prepare the spot

    We set a stable base or pan and run any needed vent, line or circuit.

  3. 3

    Set and connect

    The unit is placed, then supply lines, shutoff valves, relief valve and discharge pipe are connected.

  4. 4

    Test and review

    We fill, purge, check for leaks, bring the unit to temperature and show you the controls.

Permits for a planned install in Melrose

Melrose publishes a $40 plumbing fee for a water heater and a $40 residential fee for a gas water heater on its fee schedule. Ask Melrose Inspectional Services whether your installation needs a permit and who files it.

A licensed plumber normally pulls any needed permit, and the city says printed permits are posted at the job site. Plumbing and gas inspections run Monday to Thursday, and online review can take 2 to 3 weeks, so a planned job should allow time.

What affects the cost of an installation

Unit type and sizeTankless and hybrid models cost more than a standard storage tank.
Gas line sizingA tankless unit often needs a larger gas supply than a tank.
VentingNew runs, liners or terminations add labor and material.
Electrical workA hybrid or electric tank may need a dedicated circuit.
Permit and inspectionThe Melrose fees are small, but scheduling adds time.
