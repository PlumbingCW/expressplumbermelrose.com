Repiping in Melrose, MA

Repiping means replacing a home's worn-out water supply pipes with new ones, either the whole system or a section. It is the fix when old steel is clogging with rust, when a pipe keeps leaking in new places, or when pressure has faded throughout the house.

We handle repiping in Melrose for single-family homes, two-family houses and small buildings. Call to talk through what you are seeing, and we will say if you need a full repipe or a smaller repair.

When repairs stop being enough

One pinhole leak in a pipe is a repair. A second and third leak in the same year on the same material is a pattern. Pipes wear from the inside, and once the walls thin in one place, the rest of that run is following.

A repipe also makes sense when the symptom is not a leak at all. Rust-brown water that runs clear after a minute, or low flow at every tap and shower, often points at buildup inside the pipe. Cleaning does not fix a pipe that has narrowed with corrosion.

Pipe materials and how they age

Not every old pipe needs replacing. The material matters more than the date.

MaterialCommon troubleRepipe verdict
Galvanized steelRusts inside, narrows, leaks at threads, low flowStrong candidate once symptoms appear
LeadHealth concern, not a leak problemReplace. See water service line repair for the street side
CopperPinholes, failed solder, pitting in some casesRepair isolated leaks. Repipe if they multiply
CPVCBecomes brittle, can crack at fittingsWatch for cracks. Replace sections if they fail
PEXFitting failures, sunlight damage if exposedRarely needs repiping. Fix the fitting

  • ✓

Not all of those signs mean pipes. Sediment stirred up by city main flushing, a clogged aerator or a failing pressure regulator can look the same. Check those first, using the notes below.

Rule out the simple causes first

Melrose buys MWRA water, which is treated to reduce pipe corrosion. The city's water letter gives no hardness figure, so rust buildup inside old steel pipe and clogged aerators are both worth checking when pressure is poor throughout an older house.

The city flushes its water mains, and the 2025 round ran from September 8 into late October, and the city warned that sediment can clog home pipes, reduce pressure or discolor water. Run a cold tap until it clears, avoid hot water and laundry until it does, and take the aerators off a faucet and rinse them. If the problem is still there weeks later, call.

The city also notes lead can enter from lead solder and some brass fixtures. If your house has old soldered copper, ask us about it during a repipe, and flush taps after they sit, as the city advises.

Repipe or keep patching

Repipe

  • Ends repeat leak calls on that system
  • Restores flow and cleans up rusty water
  • Higher cost up front
  • Walls may need patching at access points

Spot repairs

  • Lower cost per visit
  • Right for one isolated failure
  • Does not fix the rest of the pipe
  • Costs add up if the leaks keep coming

If a hidden leak started the conversation, a leak detection visit can show whether it is one failure or a symptom. A single burst on old steel is covered under burst pipe repair, though it may be the warning that the rest is next.
